Section 1

Setup

Do this once. Takes 30–60 minutes. You'll never have to do it again.

Step 1

Download Claude Desktop & Get a Plan

Claude Desktop is the app that powers everything in this guide. You need the paid Pro plan to access Cowork and run automated scheduled jobs.

  • Go to claude.ai in your browser
  • Click Download Claude and install the desktop app
  • Sign in or create a free account
  • Upgrade to the Pro plan (~$34 AUD/month) — unlocks Cowork, Scheduled tasks, and extended usage
💡 Why pay? The free plan doesn't include Cowork or scheduled automation. The Pro plan is what turns Claude from a chatbot into a full job search engine running in the background for you.

Step 5

Create Your Master Resume

Before the automated search works, Claude needs a Master Resume — a complete record of your experience, skills, and achievements. It will use this to score and tailor every job application.

Open a new chat in Claude and paste the prompt below. Claude will interview you step by step and build your resume for you.

📋 Master CV Prompt — Paste into a new Claude chatYou are a senior resume strategist and technical interviewer with 10+ years of experience helping engineers build comprehensive career records. You are NOT reviewing or scoring this resume. Your single goal is to build a complete MASTER RESUME: an exhaustive record of everything about this candidate, which will later be tailored into job-specific resumes. Most users will paste a thin or incomplete resume. Your job is to find every gap and interview the user to fill it. ## THE COMPLETENESS MODEL A complete master resume must contain, with full depth: - HEADER: full name, location, phone, email, LinkedIn, GitHub/portfolio, work rights / visa status. - SUMMARY: years of experience, core domains, standout differentiators. - WORK EXPERIENCE — for EVERY role: company, title, dates, location, AND • scope (team size, scale, users, data volume, budget) • technologies used • 3-6 accomplishments, each with a metric (impact, %, time, $, scale) • leadership / ownership signals - PROJECTS: name, problem solved, your role, tech stack, outcome/metric. - SKILLS, categorized: languages, frameworks, cloud, databases, tools, AI/ML, methodologies. - EDUCATION: degree, institution, dates, honors, relevant coursework. - CERTIFICATIONS, PUBLICATIONS, TALKS, AWARDS (if any). - LINKS: anything that proves the work (repos, demos, articles). ## HOW YOU WORK (the loop) On the FIRST turn: 1. Parse whatever the user gave you. Extract everything present into the master resume structure. 2. Run a completeness audit: mark each section as COMPLETE, THIN, or MISSING. 3. Ask the FIRST batch of targeted questions — grouped by topic, max 5-7 questions. On EVERY following turn: 1. Integrate answers into the master resume. 2. Show updated completeness status. 3. Ask the next batch (5-7 max), until nothing important is missing. When complete, output the FINAL MASTER RESUME in full. ## RULES 1. Never fabricate. Leave [TO FILL: ...] placeholders. Never invent metrics, dates, or titles. 2. Push for metrics relentlessly — ask "What was the measurable result?" 3. Ask one batch at a time. Group related questions. 4. Let the user skip. If they say "N/A" or "skip," mark it and move on. 5. Dig into thin roles. A one-line job entry needs interrogation. 6. Stay an interviewer, not a critic. Extract only.
✅ After Claude finishes — save to Notion Copy the final Master Resume output and save it to a Notion page titled exactly: "Master CV/RESUME". The automated job search will look for it there every time it runs.
Section 2

Apply — Automated Job Search

Now that setup is done, this is where the magic happens. Claude searches, scores, and tracks jobs for you — automatically.

Step 6

Go to Cowork → Scheduled & Create a New Task

In the Cowork sidebar, click Scheduled. Then click + New scheduled task.

💡 What is a Scheduled Task? It's a job you configure once and Claude runs it automatically on any schedule you set — daily at 7am, every Monday, or even hourly. You don't need to be at your computer.
Step 7

Paste the Job Ranker & Generator Prompt

Copy the entire prompt below into the task's prompt field. Then update the placeholder values with your own details.

✅ You can customise this prompt however you like

This prompt is fully editable. You can tell Claude to:

  • Search only LinkedIn, only Indeed, or only SEEK
  • Save resumes as PDF to Google Drive instead of locally
  • Only return remote jobs, or only jobs above a certain salary
  • Add extra steps like drafting a cover letter for every HIGH job
  • Filter by company size, industry, or any other criteria

Claude follows your instructions. Think of this prompt as a template — it's your starting point, not a rulebook.

🤖 Job Ranker & Generator Prompt — Paste into Scheduled TaskDaily Job Search Prompt Run my daily job search. Work step by step and confirm each step in one line as you go. STEP 1 — Load profile and preferences Fetch my Master CV from Notion (page "Master CV/RESUME"). Load my search preferences (keywords, titles, experience level, job type, date range) from my preferences. Confirm in one line that both loaded. STEP 2 — Search each source SEPARATELY using the specified actors Run these three sources independently, one at a time, applying the search filters from my preferences to each. For EACH source, report how many fresh listings (posted within 7 days) it returned: - Seek — use Apify actor "blackfalcondata/seek-scraper" - LinkedIn — use Apify actor "curious_coder/linkedin-jobs-scraper" - Indeed — use indeed connector, do NOT use Apify for that Use ONLY these named actors for Seek and LinkedIn. Do NOT substitute any other scraper. If an actor errors or returns zero, say so explicitly WITH the reason. Attempt at least 7 from each source; target 20+ total. STEP 3 — Filter Remove anything that violates my dealbreakers, falls below my salary floor, or is outside my locations. Remove duplicates (same company + title) and any job already in my Notion Job Tracker. STEP 4 — Score and rank Score each remaining job 0–100 against my Master CV. Tier them HIGH (80+), MEDIUM (60–79), LOW (under 60). Sort highest first. STEP 5 — Show me the table Output ONE table, these columns only, no other prose: Title | Company | Source | Location | Salary | Score | Tier | Link STEP 6 — Log to Notion (HIGH + MEDIUM only) For each HIGH and MEDIUM job, create one row in my Notion Job Tracker 2026 with EXACTLY these fields: Title · Company · Source · Location · Salary · Score · Tier · Job Link · Date Found · Resume Variant Then print: "Logged N jobs (X skipped as duplicates)." STEP 7 — Resume variants (HIGH jobs ONLY, intelligent reuse) I maintain resume VARIANTS each targeting a role cluster (e.g. "Backend/Platform Engineer", "ML/AI Engineer", "Full-Stack", "Data Engineer"). A registry of existing variants lives in Notion DB "Resume Variants" (fields: Variant Name · Role Cluster · Key Keywords · File Path · Drive Link). For each HIGH job: 1. Classify the job into a role cluster. 2. Look up existing variants in "Resume Variants". 3. If an existing variant covers ≥85% of the job's key requirements, REUSE it. 4. If no variant fits, GENERATE one new variant from my Master CV, save as PDF to Google Drive, and save the Google Drive link in the "Resume Variants" Notion database so I can open it with one click. 5. Never generate more than one new variant per role cluster per run. 6. Do not print the resume in chat. Print: "Saved <filename> ✓" or "FAILED: <reason>". In each Notion Job Tracker 2026 row, set "Resume Variant" to the variant that applies. End with: "Reused N variants, generated M new (clusters: ...)." Preferences Nice to have: Target roles: [e.g. AI Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer, Full Stack Developer, MERN Stack, JavaScript Developer, Web Developer, Software Engineer] Experience targets: [e.g. 0-5 years] Jobs type: [e.g. Remote, Hybrid, On-site — willing to relocate anywhere in Australia] Working rights: [e.g. Full working rights] Salary range: [e.g. $70K AUD floor — up to any] Should NOT have: Target roles: [e.g. Staff AI Engineer] Experience targets: [e.g. 8+ years / very senior positions] Working rights: [e.g. Requires citizenship] My dealbreakers: [e.g. no contract roles / no relocation / no companies under 20 people]
⚠️ REQUIRED: Replace ALL Placeholder Values Before Running

Everything in [square brackets] must be replaced with your real information. The job search will not work correctly otherwise.

  • Target roles → your actual job titles (e.g. "AI Engineer, Full Stack Developer")
  • Experience targets → your actual years (e.g. "0-5 years")
  • Jobs type → your actual preference (e.g. "Remote, Hybrid — Australia")
  • Working rights → your real status (e.g. "Full working rights")
  • Salary range → your actual floor (e.g. "$70K AUD")
  • Should NOT have → your actual dealbreakers
🔴 Common mistake — don't skip this

If you leave the placeholder text in, Claude will literally search for jobs matching "[e.g. AI Engineer]" — which returns nothing. Replace every single bracket before saving the task.

Step 8

Set the Model, Schedule, and Run

Choose the model — use Haiku to keep token costs low:

Claude Haiku 4.5 Recommended — cheapest
Claude Haiku 4.6 Also great
💡 Why Haiku? This prompt is long and runs multiple steps. Haiku uses far fewer tokens than Sonnet or Opus, meaning much lower cost — especially if running daily. The quality difference for this task is negligible.

Set your schedule:

  • Daily — recommended if actively job hunting
  • Weekly — good if passively looking

Click Run to test it immediately, or save and let the schedule take over.

Section 3

Gmail Filter

When you start applying, job-related emails flood your inbox. Set up a Gmail filter so they automatically land in a "Jobs" label — then let Claude process them for you.

Step 10

Create a Gmail Filter for Job Emails

This filter automatically tags any email about jobs, applications, interviews, rejections, or offers with a "Jobs" label — keeping them organized without any manual work.

1

In Gmail, click the ⚙️ Settings gear (top right) → See all settings.

Click the gear icon → "See all settings"
2

Click the "Filters and Blocked Addresses" tab, then click "Create a new filter" at the bottom.

Go to "Filters and Blocked Addresses" tab
3

In the "Has the words" field, paste this exactly:

job OR application OR interview OR rejected OR offer OR linkedin OR seek OR indeed
Paste the keywords into "Has the words" → click "Create filter"
4

Click "Create filter". On the next screen, tick "Apply the label", then choose "New label..." from the dropdown and type Jobs.

Tick "Apply the label"
Select "Jobs" or create it as a new label
Type "Jobs" → click Create
5

Tick "Also apply filter to matching conversations" to backfill existing emails, then click Create filter.

Check "Also apply filter to matching conversations" → Create filter
✅ Done! Your Jobs label is now live Every job-related email — application confirmations, interview invites, offer letters, rejections — will automatically appear under the "Jobs" label. You'll see a count like the one below building up over time.
The Jobs label in your Gmail sidebar — all job emails in one place

Step 11

Use Claude to Process Your Jobs Label

Now that all your job emails are automatically labelled, you can tell Claude at any time to fetch them, extract key information, and log it to Notion — all with a single message.

💬 Example prompts to say to Claude"Fetch all new emails from my Jobs label in Gmail since yesterday. For each one, extract: company name, job title, current status (applied / interview invite / rejected / offer), and any action needed. Log them to my Job Tracker 2026 in Notion, matching to existing rows where possible." "Check my Jobs Gmail label and summarise what happened this week — how many applications I sent, how many interview invites I got, and any rejections. Give me a one-page summary." "Look at my Jobs Gmail label and find any emails I haven't responded to yet. Draft a reply for each one."
💡 You choose how to use it You can log emails to the same Job Tracker 2026 to update application statuses, or create a separate Notion database just for email tracking — it's completely up to you. Claude adapts to whatever structure you want.
✅ The full loop is now complete

Claude finds the jobs → scores them → logs the best ones to Notion → tailors your resume → saves it to Google Drive → and when replies start coming in, Claude reads your Gmail, updates your tracker, and drafts responses. Your entire job search, automated end to end.
